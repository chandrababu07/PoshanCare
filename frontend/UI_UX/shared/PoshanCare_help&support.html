PoshanCare utilizes the updated ICMR-NIN 2024 (Indian Council of Medical Research - National Institute of Nutrition) predictive energy equations customized for South Asian body compositions.

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is determined by age, biological sex, height, and current body weight. We apply a calibrated Physical Activity Level (PAL) factor to derive your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). If your goal includes fat loss, we apply an evidence-based metabolic deficit (typically 15-20% below TDEE) to safeguard lean muscle tissue and thyroid hormone stability.

  • Controlled Deficit: Progressive caloric reduction targeted for visceral fat loss.
